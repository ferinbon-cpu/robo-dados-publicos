# TASK 200B — School norms from primary normative authority

## Goal

Close the two school-functioning norm questions only if already-custodied primary normative evidence is strong enough. Do not force contracts, personnel acts, or budget decrees into the school-norm layer merely because they mention Education.

## Audit finding

The bounded audit of Jornal Oficial editions 7304–7315 did not produce two strong school-operation normative rows. The Education hits were dominated by personnel portarias, procurement/contracts, and budget-credit decrees. Those remain useful evidence in their own layers, but they do not prove school-functioning norms.

Two authoritative primary sources already in custody do prove substantive school-operation rules:

- CME Indicação/Deliberação 02/2021: minimum seven daily hours / 35 weekly hours, integrated daily organization, complementary activities, mandatory full-time attendance in fully full-time units, AEE, monitoring and governance.
- Municipal Decree 118/2024: institutes the municipal full-time integral education policy in municipal network schools in express consonance with CME 02/2021; official publication is recorded in JOM edition 6718 on 2024-04-20.

## Semantic correction

Historical answerability V1 is preserved unchanged. V2 changes only SCHOOL_NORMS: it now requires at least two VALIDATED PRIMARY_NORMATIVE Education rows, with both CME and MUNICIPAL_LEGISLATION represented. The recent JOM index remains enrichment, not a mandatory historical coverage gate.

Expected transition: 34 answerable / 4 partial -> 36 answerable / 2 partial. Only NORMS_Q1 and NORMS_Q2 may change. EQUITY_Q1 and TEACH_Q2 remain partial.

## Guards

Publication is not implementation. A normative act is not proof of observed compliance or effect. Contracts, personnel acts, and budget-credit decrees do not become school norms by keyword proximity.
